Copied.
Research tool. The POP score was developed retrospectively in a single centre (n = 709, 608 deaths) and has internal validation only. It has not been externally validated and is not intended to guide individual clinical decisions. Early mortality (1 and 3 months) tended to be underestimated in the highest-risk patients. Confidence intervals are approximate: bootstrap estimates from the development cohort that do not account for optimism or model selection.
